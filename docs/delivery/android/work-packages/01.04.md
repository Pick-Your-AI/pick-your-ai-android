## Outcome and exact inputs

Select one canonical TypeScript/Node API and paid PostgreSQL on Render, with
Frankfurt location, specified initial compute/storage, Pro isolation/recovery,
deliberate deployment and distinct backup/credential roles. All stateful
responsibilities have a destination or explicit deferred feature. The existing
GoDaddy website stays static; Android's guest-local J1–J4 and conditional J5 keep
their accepted dispositions. Apple #21's intended core public sharing and #23's
service requirements remain independent: canonical work for another accepted
consumer does not wait for Android remote activation or product completion.
The operating owner and recovery custodian are defined
roles with the explicit unfilled acceptance gate O1; no person, account or
ongoing duty is invented.
