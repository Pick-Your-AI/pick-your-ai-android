## Outcome and review scope

The data-inventory and threat-model outcome is complete for the planned Android program. All 15 planned flows have a purpose, recipient and trust boundary, minimization rule, access/retention disposition, and either a mitigation or an explicit unresolved enablement requirement. Eleven abuse cases trace to concrete implementation and verification work. The builder-to-share walkthrough below accounts for the planned client, OS, service and third-party transfers.

This accepts the inventory, decisions and mitigation handoff required by [#23](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-android/issues/23). It does not accept a real-data publication, installed-device security, a backend deployment, a privacy-law conclusion or a Play release. Those facts are neither assumed nor required by this issue's three documentary/design criteria; their exact implementation and release issues retain their gates.

The source reviewed is Android main [`7f3d59b5aa439180e4361f6d31761932357ff494`](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-android/tree/7f3d59b5aa439180e4361f6d31761932357ff494). The complete [inventory and threat map at that revision](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-android/blob/7f3d59b5aa439180e4361f6d31761932357ff494/docs/security/DATA_INVENTORY_AND_THREAT_MODEL.md) supplies stable F01–F15, B1–B8 and T01–T11 identifiers. Its original implementation receipt is explicitly pinned to an earlier version; the current-source reconciliation below adds the subsequently accepted controls without rewriting that earlier observation.

The live issue was refreshed on 2026-10-18: its three acceptance criteria are unchanged, it has no native delivery prerequisite, and it has no issue comments. No assignee or another person's operating commitment is invented by this record.

## Current-source reconciliation

| Flows | Accepted local behavior or enablement disposition |
| --- | --- |
| F01 workload; F02 revisions and pins; F13 configuration references | The guest preview keeps inputs in app-private `noBackupFilesDir`. Private local format 4 persists incomplete workload and typed selections, including explicit unknown/unanswered distinctions; this is not a new frozen v1 wire contract or a claim of evaluation validity. Local writes bind root, draft/revision identity and hashes before acknowledging a saved state. Old formats remain readable, and explicit migration preserves entered data. |
| F02 retained history; F03 artifact retention; T09/T11 | `PreviewDraftLibrary` supplies the reviewed per-draft limit of 128 managed artifacts and 128 MiB of managed payloads, derived from the supported 128 history entries and 1 MiB envelope limit. Orphans left before pointer activation consume the same budget. A refused new revision preserves the old head and retained pins; unchanged saves and verified reactivation remain possible. Explicit pending recovery creates a separate draft while preserving the full source. There is no eviction or application-wide storage-quota promise, and generic cache constructors remain unbounded unless configured. |
| F02 deletion and selection | Library guards precede OS locks; deletion intent invalidates stale adapters before bounded cleanup. Cleanup does not follow symbolic links, and pending failures remain actionable. Active selection is rechecked for liveness under the library lock; unavailable data cannot silently become a fresh acknowledged draft. Deletion does not promise forensic erasure or deletion of external copies. |

## Builder-to-share and abuse-case review

1. **Discover:** publisher data crosses B4 and may enter B2. Current input is synthetic. T06 requires independent publisher authority, source-use rights, complete version/hash validation and correction policy before promotion; displaying a URL is not permission to retrieve it.
2. **Configure, compare and evaluate:** F01/F02/F13 cross B1/B2. Preserve unknowns and incomplete inputs; do not infer phone-local execution or provider access. A future remote evaluation would separately cross B5 through F08 and needs a reviewed minimum payload and retention policy.
3. **Save and recover:** use durable revision/head acknowledgement. T09/T11 require stale-token, interrupted publication, corruption, quota and recovery checks. F09 independently accounts for possible backup/transfer; a local save makes no remote backup promise.
4. **Import and export:** these planned B3/B7 surfaces are disabled. T02/T03 require hostile-stream/schema tests, no overwrite before acceptance, secret sentinel tests against an allowlisted output, byte-matching preview and cleanup/grant recovery. Cancellation must not create an external transfer.
5. **Share and sync:** F06/F07/F08 cross B5/B6/B7 only after their gates. T04/T05 require object/action authorization, a scoped read-only capability, expiry/revocation, account/guest isolation and stale-outbox rejection. Knowing a build ID or verifying a domain is not write authority.
6. **Delete and operate:** distinguish local draft deletion, public cache clearing, share revocation, account deletion, backups, diagnostic retention and copies held by recipients. T08/T10 cover diagnostic/dependency/signing access; future operators and processors are explicit recipients. No accepted design promises to recall a recipient's saved copy.

The review exercised each abuse case as a design question against its current source boundary and the map's linked negative/recovery test requirement. Unsupported external flows retain explicit enablement gates; helper tests are never substituted for an installed provider, browser, service or backup test. All eight boundaries and all fifteen flows are accounted for, including implicit OS input/backup exposures and the distribution supply chain.
