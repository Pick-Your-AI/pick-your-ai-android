## Decision

Place only `docs/delivery/android/work-packages/01.05.md` in
`Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-android`, retaining the same relative path.
The [complete decision record](../delivery/android/work-packages/01.05.md) owns the
guest/account/revenue/analytics/notification dispositions, exact criterion mapping,
desk cases, policy inputs and receiving limits.

This does not relocate 01.04, 01.06, other work packages, epics or the program.
Website #15–#18 and research contract/evidence/lifecycle authorities remain
canonical. No website file, deployment, issue relationship, assignee or Project
field is changed by this record.

## Acceptance and index transition

Independent product/Android receiving review must cover the placement and full
#25 decision content, followed by normal protected delivery and exact
default-branch verification. The user's delegation supplies the authority to
make this bounded project decision; it does not invent another person's
operating role or legal/store acceptance.

This document delivery changes no criterion ledger, validator or test. After
reviewed delivery, extend the already canonical current schema through its own
independent review, using the actual delivered artifact and receiving receipt.
Preserve #25's literal IDs/text/digests, prior partial evidence and the original
website repository/path in its historical acceptance record. Preserve every
earlier accepted outcome and immutable ledger-version history. Do not create a competing
index, accept a proposed artifact as delivered, or waive the existing strict
completion checks.
