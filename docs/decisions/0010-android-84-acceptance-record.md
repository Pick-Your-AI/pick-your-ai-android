## Decision and alternatives

same relative path. It is the single criterion, implementation, failure/recovery,
prerequisite-intake and receiving record for the selected
local-retained-frozen-v1-no-external-evidence/1 client boundary.

consumer and its original native/runtime evidence from their adopted source.

together while preserving the original intended website location as history.

and canonical website/research service, contract, evidence, saved-build and
source-use authorities remain unchanged. The already selected local-interpreter
branch creates no website deployment, backend endpoint, operator commitment or
qualified external resource/cost input.

location changes. Current open native prerequisites #49/#58 and transitive #21
remain open; their available outputs need the explicitly bounded receiving
